\# 🛡️ Hifazat AI



\### Digital Safety Assistant



Hifazat AI is an AI-powered digital safety assistant designed to help users understand and respond safely to common online security and cyber-safety problems.



It provides simple, practical and responsible guidance for situations such as scams, phishing, online harassment, cyberbullying, fake profiles, privacy concerns and account-security issues.



\---



\## 🎯 Purpose



The purpose of Hifazat AI is to:



\* Protect users from common online threats

\* Provide easy-to-understand digital safety guidance

\* Help users identify suspicious online activity

\* Encourage safe reporting of serious cyber incidents

\* Promote responsible online behavior

\* Prevent users from taking unsafe or retaliatory actions



\---



\## ✨ Key Features



\### 🛡️ Digital Safety Guidance



Hifazat AI can provide guidance about:



\* Scams and phishing

\* Suspicious links

\* Online harassment

\* Cyberbullying

\* Blackmail

\* Fake profiles and impersonation

\* Account security

\* Privacy protection

\* Unauthorized image sharing

\* AI-manipulated images and deepfakes



\### 💬 AI Chat Assistant



Users can ask questions in natural language and receive practical digital-safety guidance.



\### 🔐 Security-Focused Responses



The assistant is designed not to provide:



\* Hacking instructions

\* Password bypass methods

\* OTP bypass methods

\* Malware instructions

\* Unauthorized access techniques

\* Retaliatory hacking advice

\* Private personal information



\### 🚨 Safety \& Reporting



For serious cyber incidents, users are encouraged to use appropriate official reporting channels.



\---



\## 🔒 Privacy \& Safety



Users should never share sensitive information with the assistant, including:



\* Passwords

\* OTP codes

\* CNIC numbers

\* Bank account information

\* Card information

\* Private or intimate images

\* Other confidential credentials



Hifazat AI provides general digital-safety guidance and is not a replacement for emergency services, legal advice, or official law-enforcement services.

\---



\## 🛡️ Security Approach



Hifazat AI uses multiple security measures:



1\. API credentials are kept server-side.

2\. Frontend requests are restricted through CORS configuration.

3\. Backend rate limiting helps reduce excessive requests.

4\. Request data is validated through Pydantic.

5\. AI instructions restrict harmful cyber-security assistance.

6\. Users are warned not to share sensitive credentials.

7\. The assistant does not intentionally expose private system instructions or internal configuration.
